## Follow-up review — Crossref ↔ publisher independence (same day)
**Question:** can a Crossref record and a publisher-site record corroborate each other as `independent` under D-064?
**Evidence:** D-064 (10) in `logs/DECISION_LOG.md` lists "PubMed ↔ Crossref or publisher record → `independent` only with recorded retrieval or checked-transcription provenance" and "unclassified pairs → `undetermined`". It names no Crossref ↔ publisher rule. `source_independence()` matches this: Crossref ↔ publisher reaches the final "unclassified source relationship" branch and returns `undetermined`.
**Decision:** no code change. The conservative behaviour is what the approved policy requires; making the pair `independent` would add policy. (Interpretation, not repository evidence: Crossref metadata is usually deposited by the publisher, so the two may not be independent records.)
**Tests added:** `test_crossref_publisher_pair_is_undetermined` (fully agreeing, checked pair → `undetermined`, record `partially_verified`); `test_two_urls_for_the_same_crossref_record_not_independent` (two Crossref URLs → `not_independent`; DOI-resolver URL-only row → `not_independent`).
**Documentation note (not changed):** `collection_workflow.md` §4.3.1 says "Crossref or publisher records are `independent` only with recorded retrieval or checked-transcription provenance", without the "PubMed ↔" qualifier of D-064 (10). Read as a necessary condition together with "unclassified pairs are `undetermined`", it is consistent; adding the qualifier would be a Step A documentation change and needs authorisation.
